I have been in the tech industry for over 19 years. I have worked at several major companies including IBM, State Farm, and Discover. I grew up in Bloomington-Normal, IL where I attended ISU and graduated with honors from the School of Information Technology.

None of that really matters though. What matters is that I have a passion for development and design and a perspective that comes from years of experience. After years of working in the corporate world as a technical lead and website developer, I decided to split off and do what I love most, website design.

I have a diverse background, spanning from Java and PHP development, to JavaScript/HTML5/CSS front-end work, databases/SQL, and web security; I am self taught in Photography and used to do it professionally. I am here to help with any questions/concerns you may have about your website.

Currently, I reside in Naperville, IL with my husband, my two daughters, and our dog, frogs, and fish.
